02 · About Bow Mark

A family-built company
that's been paving Alberta since 1979.

Three generations. Two trades. One promise: when Bow Mark prices a project, we deliver it. Period.

From the first asphalt lift on a rural Okotoks road in 1979 to today's highway-grade overlays and full-scale subdivision builds, we've grown by doing exactly what we said we would. Our crews self-perform paving, concrete, grading, and construction management, so there's no handoff, no finger-pointing, and no daylight between estimate and execution.

  • COR-certified safety program
