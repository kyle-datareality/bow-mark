In the Community

Okotoks is home.

The Bow Mark Group is highly involved in the community we build for. Sponsorships, teams, public infrastructure, and charitable causes across Okotoks and the foothills, year after year.

For Bow Mark and President Sean McArthur, Okotoks is home. We're proud of where we come from, and we put that pride back into the community every chance we get.
Bow Mark Paving & Concrete

01 · Teams & Sponsorships

Backing local talent.

Title sponsor

Bow Mark Oilers U-18AAA

A major and proud contributor to the U-18AAA hockey program in Okotoks. Title sponsorship of the Bow Mark Oilers is one of the longest-running commitments the company makes each season.

Bow Mark also stands behind the Okotoks Dawgs baseball team, the Okotoks Oilers, the Okotoks Bisons, and countless minor sports teams across the foothills.

02 · Events

Showing up for the big weekends.

Annual · Okotoks

Okotoks Professional Rodeo

Proud sponsor of one of the foothills' biggest annual events, drawing competitors and crowds from across western Canada.

2023 · Provincial

Alberta Summer Games

Sponsor of the 2023 Alberta Summer Games, supporting amateur athletes from every corner of the province.

Year-round

Minor sports across the foothills

Direct backing of the kids' leagues that grow into the senior teams above. Equipment, ice time, jersey sponsorships, and travel support.

03 · Community infrastructure

Building the places people gather.

Old Towne Square Plaza

McArthur Outdoor Stage

The McArthur Outdoor Stage anchors the Old Towne Square Plaza, hosting community concerts, festivals, and gatherings in the heart of Okotoks. Built to last, designed to bring people together.

Public facility

Foothills Centennial Center

Long-term support of the Centennial Center, the recreational heart of Okotoks.

Inclusive design

Bill Robertson Park Playground

Backing the all-inclusive playground at Bill Robertson Park so every kid in town has a place to play.

Arena assets

Centennial & Murray Arena Scoreboards

Scoreboards at both Centennial Arena and Murray Arena, keeping minor and adult hockey running game-night ready.

04 · Charitable support

The causes we stand behind.

End-of-life care

Foothills Country Hospice

Supporting the only freestanding rural hospice in southern Alberta, providing compassionate end-of-life care for foothills families.

Food security

Okotoks Food Bank

Year-round contributions to the Okotoks Food Bank so neighbours in need can put dinner on the table.
